One image that sums up Saturday, 21 November 2026's world news: a vintage
broadsheet front page, "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Saturday, 21 November 2026",
with ten illustrated panels for the day's ten biggest stories.

The ten stories, as reported on 21 November 2026:

1. **Missiles land in Poland** — Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone
   came down in Poland during a bombardment of Ukraine; Warsaw called it a
   deliberate provocation and NATO called it a grave violation of allied
   airspace.
2. **Cyclone Akvan hits Odisha** — Cyclonic Storm Akvan crossed India's
   east coast near Gopalpur, bringing strong winds and heavy rain.
3. **COP31 talks go into overtime** — Fossil-fuel talks deadlocked at the
   UN climate summit in Turkey, pushing negotiations past their scheduled
   close.
4. **Rikers Island to close by 2032** — New York Mayor Mamdani announced a
   plan to shut the Rikers Island jail complex by 2032.
5. **Palisades Fire arson verdict** — A Los Angeles jury convicted
   Jonathan Rinderknecht of starting the fire that became the deadly 2025
   Palisades Fire.
6. **Denmark foils assassination plot** — Danish authorities said a
   Russian plot to assassinate US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner was foiled.
7. **Bayer to pay $7.25 billion** — A judge approved a $7.25 billion Bayer
   payout to settle claims that Roundup causes cancer.
8. **Banks hit by vendor cyberattack** — JPMorgan Chase, Visa and
   Mastercard reported cybersecurity incidents involving third-party
   vendors; core payment networks were unaffected.
9. **Wall Street slumps, Sensex soars** — US stocks fell about 3.7%, the
   S&P 500 entering correction as AI shares sold off (Nasdaq down 5.28%,
   Nvidia about 14%); India's Sensex rallied nearly 898 points the same
   day. Gold fell to a 14-month low.
10. **Oldest quasar pair found** — The Webb telescope and Gemini North
    discovered the oldest known close quasar pair.

The entry image is `entry.png` (1024×683, JPEG bytes, 211,800 bytes).
`ad.png` is the same poster at full resolution (1920×1280 PNG), shown on
the ad page `index.html` with all ten stories written out. `main.py`
copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte-identical every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

That writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
Check the entry files with `python3 main.py --check`, and preview the ad
page with `python3 main.py --serve` (then open
http://127.0.0.1:8000/index.html).
